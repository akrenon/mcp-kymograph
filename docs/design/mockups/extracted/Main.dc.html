How to read a class

stable
present at ≥ 80 %, α = β = 0.05 — 14 runs with a hit
flaky
present, but not at a stable rate; rate and Wilson interval shown
absent
below 10 % at β = 0.01 — 44 runs (evidence) or 48 (sequential test)
undecided
budget spent, the data sits between the hypotheses — never new or gone in a comparison

A finding says “signal X fired at place Y”. It is a candidate for a person to look at, never a verdict on the server.
